VI. CAUSE OF ACTION
Cite the U.S. Civil Statute under which you are filing: 49 U.S.C. § 14915; 15 U.S.C. § 1125(a) (Lanham Act § 43(a)); Mississippi common law defamation and tortious interference
Brief description of cause: Defendant, a federally licensed freight broker (MC# 7263019), submitted a materially false and defamatory report to FreightGuard, a commercial carrier reporting database, falsely characterizing Plaintiff motor carrier as having caused cargo damage, abandoned a load, and altered a Bill of Lading. The report is entirely false, directly contradicted by signed delivery documentation, and has proximately caused Plaintiff to lose commercial freight contracts and suffer substantial lost revenue. Plaintiff seeks compensatory damages, punitive damages, injunctive relief requiring retraction, and attorneys' fees pursuant to applicable fee-shifting statutes.
